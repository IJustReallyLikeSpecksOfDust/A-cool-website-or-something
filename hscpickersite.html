Welcome!

This site contains a helpful tool to assist you in selecting HSC Subjects. All you have to do is select a set of a few, and the system will give you helpful information about that selection, so you can play around with a bunch of options and see what they're like!



Try it out here:

How to Use

The picker is very simple. Just search or find a subject that interests you, and click to add it to your selection. Once your done, you can see a variety of statistics and facts about your subjects, then go deeper with links or try a different group! If you log in, you can also save your selections to reconsider later.
