What do these stats mean? (use js to make this a button l8r maxi)

Units: The number of units undertaken when studying a subject. Usually two.

Area: What area of study the subject generally falls under. Named different things depending on your school.

Dropout %: How many students drop out of the subject between
year 11 and year 12. Calculated as 1 - (yr12/yr11), rounding to nearest whole number.

Inapplicable subjects have N/A used. Other subjects who could not be calculated that way were marked Nil.

Students: The number of students in year 12 who completed the subject in the year the dataset was collceted.

Mandatory: Whether a subject fulfills a requirement. Currently, this only applies to English.
